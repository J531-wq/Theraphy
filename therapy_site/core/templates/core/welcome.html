How it works

Thoughtful support,
on your own terms.

MyTherapyDoctor gives you a private space to explore what is on your mind, without the barrier of scheduling, cost, or judgment. Our AI-guided conversations are designed to help you think clearly — not to replace professional care.

Whether you are navigating a difficult relationship, supporting a child, working through past trauma, or simply need a place to put your thoughts in order, there is a conversation type here for you.
